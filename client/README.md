## What Clients Get (Platform Features & Deliverables)

Electro Navigator offers customized portals tailored to three core user types:

### 1. For EV Drivers (End Users)

* **Cross-Network Map Aggregation:** Find chargers from multiple operators in a single map view without switching apps.
* **Live Station Telemetry:** View real-time availability (Available, In-Use, Offline) and live charging power outputs.
* **Vehicle & Plug Matcher:** Filter stations strictly compatible with your car's connector type (CCS2, Type 2, CHAdeMO) and power limits.
* **EV Route Optimizer:** Plan long trips with automatic charging stops based on battery SOC (State of Charge) and terrain data.
* **Universal Charging & Wallet:** Initiate sessions via QR code/NFC and pay across different networks using a single unified balance.
* **Remote Session Monitoring:** Track charging speed (kW), energy added (kWh), and estimated time remaining directly from your lock screen.
* **Community Crowdsourcing:** Read verified driver reviews, upload station photos, and flag broken connectors.

---

### 2. For Station Operators & Partners (CPO Portal)

* **OCPP Protocol Integration:** Connect hardware directly via standard OCPP 1.6/2.0.1 web-sockets.
* **Dynamic Pricing Engine:** Set peak/off-peak rates, idle fees, and subscription discounts.
* **Remote Station Diagnostics:** Monitor charger health, execute remote reboots, and manage firmware updates.
* **Revenue Settlement:** Automated payout management and session earnings breakdowns.

---

### 3. For Platform Administrators

* **Global Monitoring Dashboard:** Real-time analytics tracking system uptime, total energy delivered, and transaction volumes.
* **Dispute & Refund Management:** Handle billing issues and user refund requests smoothly.
* **Partner Network Onboarding:** Webhooks and API integrations to bring new CPO networks online instantly.
